Build order - each step gated (HANDOFF section 14)

Nothing below step 1 is meaningful until get_throttled reads 0x0. F1 is the root cause; rebuilding wiring on a sagging rail just produces better-looking intermittency.
  1. 1Power
    vcgencmd get_throttled reads 0x0 and stays there for an hour with both MQ heaters on. Meter pins 2/6: below 4.75 V is the fault.
  2. 2Config + clock
    sudo scripts/pi_config.sh · fit the CR2032 · reboot · sudo hwclock -w · /dev/rtc0 exists · hwclock -r is right.
  3. 3Protoboard
    Header, ground strip and rails first; continuity-check every net before any IC goes on. Then R1-R6, C1, C2, terminals, labels.
  4. 4Services
    sudo systemctl restart hics-core hics-web; the log shows Logged to DB ... | NULL: ... naming exactly the disconnected channels. gate_check.py G6.
  5. 5One sensor at a time
    I2C trio -> ADC + soil -> DS18B20 -> DHT22 -> MQ pair, tests/test_all.py --skip camera after each.
  6. 624 h on the bench
    ~1440 rows for a day, throttled still 0x0. Closes F5 - the station has never done this.
  7. 7Lid, glands, seal
    Bond standoffs (sheet 4); three PG7 holes + vent in one side wall (wall template); silicone cures 24 h. The lid is not drilled.
  8. 8Mast and pod
    Shield, pod, rain plate on the PVC; solder + heatshrink at every sensor; drip loops.
  9. 9Roof
    Box on the tile under the hood table, mast clamped, probes in the biggest vase. 48 h clean data before going public.
